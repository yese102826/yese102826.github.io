Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 29, 2026

Thank you for using "Senior Call" (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains that our App is designed with privacy as a priority and does not collect, store, or share your personal data with any external servers.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect any personal data.

The App functions entirely offline. The names and phone numbers you enter into the App for the three buttons are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). This data is strictly used to display the button names and to execute the dialing command when a button is tapped.

We do not have access to this data, nor is it transmitted to us or any third parties.

2. Permissions

The App initiates phone calls using the iOS system dialer. When you tap a button, the App simply hands off the phone number to the iOS Phone app. The App does not listen to your calls, record audio, or access your call history.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No user behavior data is tracked or sent to third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
