隐私政策

最后更新：2026年2月

一、概述

本应用尊重您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何对待您在使用本应用时可能涉及的数据，以及我们如何遵守适用法律（包括 Apple 的 App Store 与隐私相关要求）。

二、我们收集或使用的数据

2.1 仅存储在您设备上的数据（本地数据）

以下数据仅保存在您的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器或任何第三方服务器：

  • 游戏分数、历史最高分、今日最高分
  • 连续游玩天数等统计信息
  • 是否已看过玩法演示等偏好设置

上述数据用于为您提供游戏功能与记录，您卸载应用后即会随应用数据一起清除。

2.2 广告相关数据

本应用使用 Google AdMob 展示可选激励视频广告（例如在「再玩一局」时您可选择观看广告以继续游戏）。在此过程中：

  • 我们会在征得您同意的前提下，通过 Apple 的「App 追踪透明度」（ATT）框架请求追踪权限；您可以选择「允许」或「不允许」。
  • 若您选择「允许」，广告合作方可能使用设备标识符（如 IDFA）用于个性化广告与效果衡量；若您选择「不允许」，我们仍会展示广告，但不会基于跨应用追踪进行个性化。
  • AdMob 可能收集与广告展示、点击相关的数据，其处理方式受 Google 隐私政策约束。Google 隐私政策

我们不会将您的本地游戏数据（如分数）提供给广告方。

三、我们不会做的事

  • 不要求您注册账号或登录
  • 不向第三方出售您的个人数据
  • 不收集您的位置、通讯录、相册等与游戏无关的敏感信息

四、数据安全与存储

本地数据仅存储在您设备上的应用沙盒内，由系统与我们的实现保护。我们不会在自有服务器上存储您的游戏数据。

五、儿童与适用年龄

本应用未针对 13 岁以下儿童设计并主动收集其个人信息。若您认为我们意外收集了儿童信息，请通过下方联系方式告知，我们将尽快删除。

六、政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。重大变更会在应用或应用商店页面中说明，继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的政策。

七、联系我们

如对本隐私政策或数据处理有疑问，请通过您在 App Store 上看到的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Overview

the App respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle data that may be involved when you use the App, and how we comply with applicable requirements (including Apple App Store and privacy-related rules).

2. Data We Collect or Use

2.1 Data Stored Only on Your Device (Local Data)

The following data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers:

  • Game scores, best score, today's best score
  • Statistics such as consecutive play days
  • Preferences such as whether you have seen the gameplay demo

This data is used to provide game features and records. It is removed when you uninstall the App.

2.2 Advertising-Related Data

The App uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (e.g. you may choose to watch an ad to play again). In that context:

  • We may request tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework with your consent; you can choose "Allow" or "Ask App Not to Track".
  • If you allow, our ad partner may use device identifiers (such as IDFA) for personalized ads and measurement; if you decline, we will still show ads but will not use cross-app tracking for personalization.
  • AdMob may collect data related to ad delivery and interaction, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not provide your local game data (e.g. scores) to advertisers.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not collect sensitive information unrelated to the game (e.g. location, contacts, photos)

4. Security and Storage

Local data is stored only in the App's sandbox on your device and is protected by the system and our implementation. We do not store your game data on our own servers.

5. Children and Age

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us using the details below and we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in the App or on the app store listing. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please use the developer contact information shown on the App Store listing.
